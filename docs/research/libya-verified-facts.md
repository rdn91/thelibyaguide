# Libya: verified facts for TheLibyaGuide (Phase 1 research)

Compiled 2026-11-08. All URLs accessed 2026-11-08 unless stated.
Confidence: **H** = primary/official source read directly; **M** = reputable secondary or several consistent sources; **L** = single operator/blog/aggregator, or conflicting sources.
**STALE?** = source older than 12 months (before Nov 2025).
Framing tags: **[OUTDATED]** misconception that really is outdated · **[REAL]** current risk · **[UNKNOWN]** unknown or contested.

Caveat on sources: much of the visa/practical detail comes from tour operators (Koryo, Saiga, Against the Compass, Visamundi) who sell trips. Their safety claims are their own and marked as such.

### 1.2 FCDO region by region (GOV.UK regional-risks page, https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/libya/regional-risks, current at 8 Nov 2026) **H**
- **Tripoli**: all but essential. Relatively stable since the Oct 2020 UN-brokered ceasefire. Rival militias control different districts. The most recent inter-militia fighting was **May 2025**, and fighting "can break out without warning".
- **Benghazi / Al-Bayda / Derna / Jebel Akhdar**: all but essential. "Calm through 2024 and 2025". A single military group is in control. Risk of in-fighting remains. No incidents reported in the last 12 months.
- **Misrata**: all but essential. Stable. Its security directorate is "widely considered the most professional in the western region".
- **Everywhere else = against ALL travel.** That includes **Sabratha, Al-Khums/Leptis Magna, Zliten, the Nafusa mountains (Gharyan, Yefren, Nalut, Kabaw), Ghadames, Zuwara, all of Fezzan, and Tobruk**. Location inference from the FCDO boundary list. **H**
- FCDO safety page (https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/libya/safety-and-security): Daesh is responsible for most attacks; Fezzan is "a major base" for militant groups; Western nationals are seen as high-value hostages; no terrorist kidnap for many years, but 4 foreigners were kidnapped in SE Libya in 2018; kidnap for ransom by militias is more likely in northern coastal cities; the coastal road **Zawiya–Tripoli is "very high" risk**; fuel shortages, summer power cuts, and airport/road closures are common. Aug 2023 clashes temporarily closed Mitiga. **H**
- Canada: travel **outside Tripoli needs prior authorisation from Libyan authorities** (except official border crossings to Egypt/Tunisia), and checkpoints may otherwise refuse passage. The Algeria border is closed; the Egypt/Tunisia borders can close at short notice. Avoid overland travel in the south-east. (GAC, 26 May 2026) **H**

### 1.4 What has actually improved (with dates)
- **[OUTDATED] "Libya is an active war zone everywhere."** There has been no nationwide war since the Oct 2020 ceasefire, and FCDO describes Benghazi and Misrata as calm/stable through 2024–25. (FCDO) **H**
- **[OUTDATED] "You can't get a tourist visa."** The tourist e-visa has existed since **21 Mar 2024**; one operator reports a 100% approval rate from 2025. (Wikipedia Visa policy of Libya; Against the Compass) **M**
- Organised group tours are running on a published calendar: Koryo (Dec 2026 departure, applications due 15 Nov 2026), Rocky Road (Nov 2026), Mzungu (14–20 Nov 2026, includes Acacus), Kumakonda (14–24 Nov 2026, Fezzan), Responsible Vacation (2026–27 dates). Koryo says western Libya has run "without serious incident for several years" (operator claim). **M** for existence, **L** for the safety claims.
- Flights: ITA Airways **resumed Rome FCO–MJI from 3 Sep 2026**, 2×/week A319, AZ868/869 (AeroRoutes, 27 Apr 2026, https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260427-azsep26mji; Libya Observer). Aeroitalia schedules MJI from Milan Bergamo and Rome (AeroRoutes, 8 Sep 2026). Pegasus Istanbul SAW–MJI daily from 5 Oct 2026 (AeroRoutes, 2 Sep 2026). Air Cairo Cairo–MJI from May 2026. Royal Air Maroc Casablanca–MJI was planned for Jun 2026 and then moved to 4Q26 (AeroRoutes, 6 May 2026). **M** (schedule announcements; operation not confirmed).
- ICAO airspace normalisation, 23 Oct 2026 (see 1.1). **M**
- UNMAS removed 57,203 explosive remnants of war and released 1.28 M m² of land in 2025 (UNMAS programme page, data Feb 2026, https://unmas.org/en/node/132489). **M**
- Derna: post-Storm-Daniel (Sep 2023, ~4,000 dead) reconstruction includes 9 bridges, housing, and a hospital; "getting back on its feet" (AFP via Arab News/Africanews, 6 Jun 2026). **M**

## 2. Tourist visas and entry

- **E-visa** launched **21 Mar 2024**. Tourist e-visa: single entry, valid 90 days, stay up to 30 days, **US$63** (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Libya). Visamundi (updated 21 Oct 2026, https://www.visamundi.co/en/destinations/libya/) gives ~US$63 single / ~US$208 multiple entry. **M**
- **Official portal**: evisa.gov.ly (TLS certificate error on fetch 8 Nov 2026). Visamundi says the "Wafed" and "E-Visa Libya" platforms became the only official channels in Jun 2026. **L**. Confirm which URL is canonical before the site links it.
- **Sponsor / LOI is mandatory**: "citizens of all countries require a tour operator or local sponsor"; no e-visa without one (Wikipedia; Visamundi; Koryo, Jun 2026, https://koryogroup.com/blog/how-to-get-visa-for-libya). **H/M**
- **Processing**: official 3–5 business days (Visamundi); 2–4 weeks from LOI to approval (Koryo, onestep4ward); ~5 working days (Against the Compass). Canada says embassy visas take ~20 working days, in person, residents only. **M**. The site's "budget 4–6 weeks" is conservative but defensible.
- **Visa on arrival SUSPENDED**: a GNU circular dated 9 Jun 2026 (published 11 Jun) suspended visa-on-arrival at airports, and per Libya Herald and Visamundi also at land borders, pending a review. E-visa not affected (Visamundi). (Libya Herald, Jun 2026; Xinhua, 11 Jun 2026; Libya Observer) **H** that it happened; **[UNKNOWN]** whether it has been lifted.
- **Eastern government (Benghazi)** banned entry for nationals of Sudan, Eritrea, Ethiopia and Somalia on 23 Jun 2026 (Reuters via US News). This is a separate authority. **M**
- **Benghazi / east needs a separate permit**: Koryo says the "Benghazi entry permit" is **US$600 pp**, often not included in tour prices (Jun 2026). Royal Jordanian says an Entry Approval Letter is issued by the Benghazi Police Department and is valid 30 days. A forum commenter puts it at $300–400. FCDO notes a visa issued overseas "may not be recognised in some areas". **M** that a permit exists; **L** on the price.
- **Arabic passport translation**: introduced in Nov 2007 (US State Dept archive). Temehu says it was abolished for tourist visas in 2010 (**L**). Visamundi (Oct 2026) says the e-visa portal no longer lists it but the US State Dept page still mentions it, and recommends a sworn translation as a cheap precaution. FCDO entry page does not mention it. **[UNKNOWN]**: tell travellers to ask their operator/airline.
- **Israel rule**: Israeli citizens banned from entry and transit; any evidence of travel to Israel → refusal (Wikipedia; FCDO "will not be accepted"; Koryo; Canada "may lead to denied entry"). **H**
- **Passport validity**: Canada and Koryo say 6 months beyond travel; FCDO says valid for the whole stay; lost/stolen-reported passports refused. **H**
- **Arrival**: the operator's rep meets you before immigration; you cannot enter without them (Koryo; Against the Compass). **M**
- **Police registration within 7 days** of arrival, usually done by the host or agency; failing to register risks a fine on departure (FCDO entry page, https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/libya/entry-requirements). **H**
- **Tourist-police escort**: required on operator trips (Wikipedia; Against the Compass). One 2025 commenter says walking alone in Tripoli is no longer allowed; itineraries are shared with security and are fixed. **M**
- **Cash-on-entry rule**: Wikipedia says tourists must hold ≥1,000 LYD, with exemptions for paid tour packages. **L**
- **Desert/south**: Canada says authorisation is needed for any travel outside Tripoli. Fezzan tours (Acacus, Ubari, Ghat) are advertised for Nov 2026 (Mzungu, Kumakonda), but the permit mechanics are not documented by any official source found. Against the Compass reports a tourist detained ~2 months for visiting Ubari lakes in **2022**. **[UNKNOWN]**
- Visa-exempt: Algeria, Tunisia, Mauritania (restored 30 Aug 2026), Malaysia (8 Jun 2026); conditional for Egypt and Turkey age groups; Jordanians get visa on arrival at MJI/Misrata (status after Jun 2026 unclear). (Wikipedia) **M**

Suggested copy:
> "Libya has issued tourist e-visas since 2024, but only with sponsorship from a licensed Libyan operator, and eastern Libya needs its own permit on top. Rules changed twice in 2026 alone, so treat your operator and the official portal as the only sources of truth."

## 3. Practicalities

### Flights and airports
- **MJI (Mitiga)** is Tripoli's working airport. Tripoli International (TIP) has been closed since 2014. Carriers serving MJI in 2026: Turkish (IST), Pegasus (SAW, from Oct 2026), ITA (FCO, from Sep 2026), Aeroitalia, Air Cairo, Tunisair and Libyan Wings (Tunis), Libyan Airlines (Tunis, Sfax, Istanbul, Alexandria, Benghazi), Royal Air Maroc (planned 4Q26). (AeroRoutes; Against the Compass; airportroutes.com Jul 2026) **M**
- **BEN (Benina)** and **MRA (Misrata)**: Turkish Airlines' booking page lists IST–BEN and IST–MRA. Domestic links via Libyan Airlines, Afriqiyah, Berniq, Libyan Wings and MedSky. **M**
- One traveller (Feb 2026) reports the airline initially accepted only Libyan passengers, and the group needed special permission (~3 days of calls). Libyan carriers are hard to book from abroad; Libyan Wings tickets in Tunis were cash-only at an office. **L**
- Libyan carriers remain on the EU Air Safety List (banned from EU airspace since 2014). **M, not re-verified for 2026.**

### Money
- **Official rate ~6.40–6.44 LYD/USD** (CBL, 3 Nov 2026). Devalued twice in under a year, including −14.7% in Jan 2026. **Parallel rate ~10.2–10.3 LYD/USD** (15 Oct 2026), a ~55% premium; the World Bank's H1 2026 average premium was 42%. (Rio Times, 15 Oct 2026, https://www.riotimesonline.com/libyan-dinar-parallel-market-above-10-per-dollar-2026/ ; Libya Herald Sep 2026) **M**. Volatile; never print a fixed rate.
- **Cash only**: foreign cards are generally not accepted and ATMs are rare (Canada; FCDO; Against the Compass; onestep4ward). **H**
- Exporting LYD is illegal. Declare foreign cash over US$10,000 (FCDO); Canada says declare all foreign currency on entry and exit or risk confiscation. Official exchange accepts only USD, EUR, GBP, CHF and TND (Canada). **H**
- Prices (2024–25 anecdote): local meal 25–40 LYD, espresso 2–5 LYD (Against the Compass). **L**

### Connectivity
- Local SIM at the airport: ~39 LYD for 20 GB (Against the Compass) or ~$10 (onestep4ward, Feb 2026); 4G/5G reported. Operators are Libyana and Al-Madar (general knowledge, not re-verified). **M/L**
- Some content is censored; a VPN is recommended (Against the Compass). **L**
- Summer power cuts are common (FCDO). **H**

### Laws and customs
- **Alcohol illegal** since 1969 (FCDO; Against the Compass). **H**
- Ramadan: no eating, drinking or smoking in public by day. Proselytising is illegal. Same-sex activity is illegal (up to 5 years). No public displays of affection. Carry your passport at all times. (FCDO) **H**
- Dress: conservative. Most women wear hijab, many wear niqab (Against the Compass). Covered shoulders and knees for all, and headscarves for women in mosques, is the standard advice. **M**
- **Photography**: permission is required at or near military facilities (FCDO). Avoid checkpoints, police, government buildings and oil sites (standard practice; guides advise asking). **H** for military; **M** for the rest.
- Keep a low profile, avoid political discussion, and don't post itineraries on social media (FCDO; onestep4ward). **H**

### Insurance
- Standard policies are unlikely to cover Libya, and travelling against FCDO advice can invalidate insurance (FCDO; Smartraveller). **H**
- Specialist cover: Against the Compass says IATI is the only insurer they found that covers Libya (**L**, affiliate link). Koryo says specialist high-risk cover with medevac is mandatory on its tours. Other high-risk providers exist but their Libya policy wordings were not checked. **[UNKNOWN]** at provider level.

Suggested copy:
> "Bring enough clean US dollars or euros for the whole trip, because cards and ATMs won't work. Buy specialist insurance that names Libya and covers medical evacuation. There's no alcohol, dress is modest, and you should never photograph anything military."

## 5. Local mode (domestic travel for Libyans)

Sourcing here is thin. No official domestic-tourism statistics were found. **Mostly L.**
- **Beaches**: Janzour (west Tripoli) is busy with families at weekends; Zuwara is quieter; Benghazi's El-Hamra/Juliana beaches are busiest in Jul–Aug; Ras al-Hilal, Susa/Apollonia and Tolmeita are Green Mountain coastline with limited facilities (opentravelguide, IntoLibya, tourinlibya). **L**
- **Garabulli**: Garabulli/Kouf-area dunes, lagoons and coast. Nothing found on current resort operations. **L**
- **Mountain getaways**: Gharyan (troglodyte houses, ~1 h from Tripoli), Yefren, Nalut and Kabaw in the west; Al-Bayda, Shahat, Wadi al-Kuf and Ras al-Hilal in the east. The Green Mountain is the classic summer escape (cooler, with forests). **M** (consistent across guides)
- **Wadi Kaam** (Zliten/Khoms) is used for family camping and barbecues. **L**
- **Seasons**: coast/mountains are best Oct–May. Swimming season is May/Jun–Oct, with the peak Jul–Aug. Sahara Nov–Mar; avoid Jun–Aug (>40°C). (Against the Compass; Mzungu) **M**
- **Ghadames International Festival**: traditionally a 3-day Tuareg/Ghadamsi heritage festival in **Oct–Nov** (some sources say mid-Oct; 2006 was in Nov). **No confirmed 2025 or 2026 dates found.** (Temehu calendar; tourslibya.com) **L, STALE?**
- **Ghat/Acacus festival (Dec–Jan)** and **Nalut spring festival**: historically listed (Temehu). 2025–26 editions not confirmed. **L, STALE?**
- Domestic practicalities that matter: East–West checkpoints and separate authorities; domestic flights MJI–BEN (Libyan Airlines, Berniq, Afriqiyah, plus Turkish connections); summer power cuts and fuel shortages (FCDO); the Zawiya–Tripoli coastal road is high-risk (FCDO, Canada).
- Domestic risk framing: Libyans are not covered by Western advisories in the same way, but the same incident data applies (Zawiya May 2026, Tripoli protests Jul 2026, Qatrun Sep 2026).

Suggested copy:
> "Weekend escapes like Janzour beach, Gharyan, the Nafusa villages and the Green Mountain are part of normal Libyan life. Check road conditions and local news before a long drive, especially on the western coastal road and anywhere in the south."

## 6. Framing summary

**(a) Genuinely outdated**
- "There's a nationwide war." No: there has been a ceasefire since Oct 2020, and the east and Misrata are described as calm (FCDO).
- "No tourist visas." E-visa since Mar 2024.
- "No flights." MJI, BEN and MRA all operate, and ITA, Aeroitalia, Pegasus, Air Cairo and Turkish have added or resumed routes in 2026.
- "Ghadames is endangered heritage." Removed from the UNESCO danger list in Jul 2025.

**(b) Real and current**
- All major Western advisories are at the top tier, and the US reaffirmed Level 4 on 31 Aug 2026.
- Sudden armed clashes: Tripoli May 2025; Zawiya May 2026; a gunfight on the Leptis–Tripoli road in Feb 2026; Saif al-Islam's assassination in Zintan, Feb 2026.
- Kidnap-for-ransom risk in coastal cities; militant presence in Fezzan.
- No US, Canadian or Australian embassy, so consular help is very limited.
- Insurance exclusions.
- A cash-only economy with a 55% parallel-rate premium.
- Power and fuel shortages.
- UXO: 57k items removed in 2025; 52 deaths in 2025 (HRS citing UNMAS, **M**).

**(c) Unknown or contested**
- Whether visa-on-arrival has been restored.
- The Arabic translation rule.
- The Benghazi permit price and whether the east is consistently open.
- The legal mechanics of Fezzan permits.
- Whether the Qatrun southern-border closure is still in force.
- Ghadames festival dates.
- The UNSMIL mandate renewal outcome.
